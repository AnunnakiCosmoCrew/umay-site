What happened in this call

What Umay does on every call, shown in the words of the call above.

"Ben Umay, yapay zeka asistanıyım.""I'm Umay, an AI assistant."
It says it is an AI in its first sentence, and never denies it when asked.
"Perşembe 14.00 uygun görünüyor.""Thursday 2 pm is free."
It checks your calendar and offers only times that are free.
"sıfır, beş beş beş, iki dört altı…""zero, five five five, two four six…"
It reads the name back, the phone number digit by digit and the plate letter by letter, and confirms before anything is saved.
"Randevunuz oluşturuldu.""Your appointment is booked."
It finishes the job inside the call: the booking, record or lookup happens in your own system.
Not in this call
When a question isn't covered by your information, it doesn't guess. It passes the call to your staff.

Other kinds of work

Besides bookings, the same agent takes a fault report with a number, looks up a status, and says plainly what is a staff matter.

What happened in these calls

"Kaydınız oluşturuldu, numarası A R Z, sıfır sıfır bir.""Your report is filed, number A R Z, zero zero one."
It listens to a fault or complaint, asks where and since when, reads it back and opens the record only after a yes. It never makes up the number: it comes from the record system. If danger is described it opens no record and sends the caller to a person.
"Aracınız şu an serviste, fren balata değişimi işlemi sürüyor.""Your car is in the workshop now, the brake pad change is under way."
It gives a status only by record number and only what your own system returns. It never states a status, time or amount the system did not give.
"Tahmin yürütüp size yanlış bir şey söylemek istemem.""I won't guess and risk telling you something wrong."
On something not in your information, or specific to one customer, it does not make anything up; it says plainly that this is a staff matter. Connecting the call to a staff member is still in development: in this call the caller is not put through to anyone.
